## 🚨 Today's Lead Story  
### Explainer: What Are 'Large Language Models'?  
As AI research accelerates, large language models (LLMs) have emerged as the flagship technology in natural‐language processing. Built on transformer architectures introduced in 2017, LLMs leverage multi-head self-attention mechanisms to weigh relationships between words across massive text corpora—web pages, books and articles. By optimizing billions to trillions of parameters, models such as OpenAI’s GPT series, Google’s PaLM and Meta’s LLaMA generate fluent text, translate languages, draft code and even create poetry.  

Key developments:  
- **Scaling Gains:** Parameter counts have swelled from tens of millions to trillions, driving benchmark performance but at steep computational and energy costs.  
- **Hallucinations & Bias:** Models can produce confident yet inaccurate or biased outputs, posing risks in law, medicine and journalism.  
- **Ethical & Environmental Concerns:** Training a single state-of-the-art LLM can emit hundreds of tons of CO₂, while data provenance and user privacy remain unresolved.  
- **Toward Safer AI:** Research efforts focus on efficiency (sparse attention, distillation), interpretability tools and alignment methods such as reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF).  

As businesses integrate LLMs into chatbots, coding assistants and educational tools, stakeholders must balance innovation with robust governance—ensuring transparency, accountability and ethical guardrails.
